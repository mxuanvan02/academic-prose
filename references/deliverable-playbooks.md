# Vietnamese Academic Deliverable Playbooks

Apply the shared claim-evidence and terminology contracts to every Vietnamese
academic deliverable. The medium changes information density and sequencing;
it does not relax semantic integrity, scientific stance, or language quality.

## Automatic Routing

Automatically activate `academic-vi` when Vietnamese content has an academic,
scientific, research, scholarly, or higher-education purpose. Route by purpose,
not by extension: a slide deck, worksheet, script, or web page can all carry
academic discourse. Do not activate solely because ordinary Vietnamese text
mentions a technical topic; the communicative purpose must be academic or
instructional at an appropriate disciplinary level.

Nói cách khác, academic-vi phải **tự động kích hoạt** theo **mục đích học thuật**,
kể cả khi văn bản tiếng Việt chỉ là một lớp nội dung trong sản phẩm lớn hơn.

## Ownership Boundary

academic-vi chịu trách nhiệm về kiến trúc lập luận, trạng thái bằng chứng,
thuật ngữ, văn phong chuyên ngành, cách giải thích và câu chữ tiếng Việt. Công
cụ định dạng chịu trách nhiệm về phân cấp thị giác, bố cục, phân trang, hoạt
ảnh và xuất tệp. Việc rút gọn nội dung cho một định dạng trực quan không làm
suy giảm phạm vi của tuyên bố, điều kiện giới hạn, quan hệ quy thuộc, tính toàn
vẹn của số liệu hoặc ranh giới giữa bằng chứng và diễn giải.

## Slides And Speaker Notes

- Give each slide one communicative function and one dominant claim.
- Use the title to state the slide's point when evidence supports it; avoid
  generic labels that hide the argument.
- Keep evidence, quantities, units, source anchors, and necessary qualification
  adjacent to the claim they support.
- Write concise on-slide text without turning fragments into ambiguous claims.
- Put elaboration, transitions, caveats, and oral explanation in lời thuyết trình
  or speaker notes; notes must not contradict or overstate the slide.
- Preserve the argument arc across slides: question -> basis -> analysis ->
  result -> bounded implication.

## Teaching Content And Course Materials

For bài giảng and học liệu, profile the learner level, prior knowledge,
discipline, learning outcomes, session duration, and assessment conditions.
Sequence content as prior knowledge -> concept -> worked example -> guided
practice -> independent application -> synthesis. Distinguish established
knowledge, interpretation, example, and simplification. A pedagogical
simplification must remain accurate within its stated scope.

For a đề cương, align outcomes, content, learning activities, and assessment.
Use observable verbs only when the intended learner performance is actually
assessed. Do not claim that an activity develops or measures a competence
without an explicit alignment argument.

## Assessment Items

For câu hỏi đánh giá, state the construct, learning outcome, cognitive demand,
source scope, answer key, and rationale before wording the item. Ensure the key
is supported, distractors are plausible within the learner level, and no
irrelevant linguistic cue reveals the answer. Structural validity does not
establish legal, clinical, scientific, or psychometric validity; require human
review for consequential use.

## Deliverable Gate

Apply the six common rubric dimensions to both the whole deliverable and each
content layer. Additionally verify that:

1. compression has not removed a necessary condition or qualification;
2. slide titles, body text, visuals, notes, and narration make compatible claims;
3. teaching explanations match the declared learner level without distortion;
4. learning outcomes, activities, and assessment are aligned;
5. citations and protected values remain attached to the claims they support.
