# Vietnamese Academic Genre Playbooks

Use these as rhetorical move sets, not mandatory templates.

Move identifiers are defined in [Rhetorical Move Registry](rhetorical-moves.md).
Writing failures are defined in [Academic Writing Failure Taxonomy](writing-failure-taxonomy.md).

## Abstract

Establish the problem or gap -> state purpose -> identify method/material -> report principal result -> state bounded contribution. Do not add background, claims, or implications absent from the study.

## Introduction

Define the research territory -> identify a consequential and evidenced gap -> delimit the problem -> state purpose/question -> preview the contribution without promotional inflation. A gap is not merely “few studies exist”; explain what remains unresolved and why it matters.

## Literature synthesis

Organize by problem, construct, method, finding pattern, or disagreement. Compare sources through a common analytic dimension. Do not produce an author-by-author catalogue. Distinguish consensus, convergence, tension, and absence of evidence.

## Methods

State design and rationale -> context/sample/material -> procedure -> measures -> analysis -> decision rules and ethics where applicable. Favor reproducible operational detail. Do not infer rationale when none is supplied.

## Results

Orient the reader to the analysis -> report findings in question order -> provide quantities and uncertainty -> identify patterns without importing explanations reserved for Discussion.

## Discussion

Answer the research question -> interpret results -> connect or contrast with supplied literature -> consider alternatives -> delimit inference -> state implications proportional to evidence. Keep result, interpretation, and implication distinguishable.

## Conclusion

Synthesize the answer and contribution -> delimit applicability -> identify warranted next work. Do not repeat the abstract or broaden the claim.

## Thesis or report section

State the section's function in the larger argument, maintain terminology and claim continuity across chapters, and use summaries to integrate rather than duplicate content.
