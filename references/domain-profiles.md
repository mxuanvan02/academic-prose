# Domain Profiles

Profiles tune terminology and discourse; they never override the fidelity contract.

## Shared Intake

Record discipline, subfield, genre, section, intended readers, locale/institutional convention, and whether English terms should appear at first mention.

## Natural Sciences and Engineering

- Prefer operational verbs and explicit variables, conditions, mechanisms, and uncertainty.
- Preserve distinctions among measurement, estimation, simulation, verification, and validation.
- Do not humanize systems (`mô hình hiểu`) unless the field convention warrants it.

## Biomedicine and Health

- Preserve population, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, time, dosage, and adverse-event scope.
- Never convert association to causation or statistical significance to clinical importance.
- Use authoritative Vietnamese terminology where established; retain an English term at first mention when ambiguity remains.

## Social Sciences and Education

- Preserve construct definitions, level of analysis, participant voice, and qualitative versus quantitative inference.
- Distinguish generalizability, transferability, association, mechanism, perception, and reported experience.
- Avoid administrative Vietnamese that obscures actors and social relations.

## Economics, Management, and Policy

- Identify whether a statement is descriptive, causal, normative, forecast, or recommendation.
- Preserve institutional jurisdiction, stakeholder, time horizon, and policy conditionality.
- Do not strengthen `should consider` into a mandatory prescription.

## Law and Legal Studies

- Preserve legal actor, authority, modality, condition, exception, jurisdiction, and document status.
- Prefer terminology from the relevant legal system; false friends across jurisdictions require a note.
- Do not paraphrase quoted legal language as though it were the operative text.

## Computing and AI

- Distinguish model, method, protocol, system, pipeline, benchmark, task, metric, and endpoint.
- Preserve train/test/evaluation boundaries, baseline/control distinctions, and deterministic/probabilistic claims.
- Keep established model and dataset names unchanged.

## Genre Adjustments

- **Abstract**: compact problem-method-result-contribution chain; no details absent from the paper.
- **Introduction**: calibrated gap and contribution; no promotional inflation.
- **Methods**: reproducible sequence, parameters, materials, and decision rules.
- **Results**: report observations without explanation unless explicitly integrated.
- **Discussion**: separate result, interpretation, comparison, implication, and limitation.
- **Conclusion**: do not broaden scope beyond the evidence established earlier.
