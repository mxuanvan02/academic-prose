# Introduction

This paper presents a method for estimating label reliability. The evaluation
covers three datasets so that the estimate is not specific to one collection.

# Method

Each stage is ablated to establish which components carry the result. Records
were retrieved from Scopus and Web of Science, and the search strings are listed
in Appendix A.

# Results

Accuracy reaches 0.847 on a held-out set of 1,204 items. The interquartile range
of per-item scores is 0.12, and the first quartile falls at 0.79. Revenue in Q1
of the observation window was excluded because the series begins mid-quarter.

# Discussion

The impact of annotator disagreement is bounded by the reliability estimate
rather than by the size of the corpus.
