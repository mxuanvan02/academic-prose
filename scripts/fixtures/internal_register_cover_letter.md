# Cover letter

Dear Editor,

We submit "Estimating label reliability under partial annotation" for
consideration. The manuscript establishes that reliability can be bounded
without a full second pass of annotation.

The work fits this journal's scope because the readership works directly on
annotation protocols, and the journal has published related measurement work.
We are aiming to publish in a venue whose readers can act on the protocol
change, which is why we chose this section rather than a general venue.

All declarations are complete and the manuscript is not under concurrent
review.
